Quick answers

Common questions before you start

Everything you need to know about connecting platforms, reports, and data protection.

What access does Open Podcast need?

Read-only access – you never share passwords. For Apple Podcasts, add our email address with the “Analyst” role. We send exact instructions after signup.

Which platforms can I connect?

Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, and Podigee. Data from additional podcast hosts can also be integrated.

When will my first report be ready?

Usually within 24 hours after you connect your platforms.

What data does the report contain?

Downloads, plays, listeners, listening time, retention, apps and devices, demographics, countries and regions, plus individual episode development.

Can I customize the design and pages?

Yes. Podcast Report includes three color templates. Agency plans can also customize the logo, colors, typeface, included pages, and page order.

How often can I create reports?

As often as you like and for any time range. You can also receive reports automatically by email every week or month.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in Germany and according to GDPR standards. We never sell your podcast data to third parties.

Can I cancel or pause my account?

Yes. You can cancel monthly, pause the account, or switch plans at any time.
